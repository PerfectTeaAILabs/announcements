# PerfectTea Announcements

**Official announcements from PerfectTea and its projects.**

This repository is dedicated exclusively to official announcements, updates, and important notices from **PerfectTea** and its ecosystem.

## Purpose

PerfectTea Announcements serves as a centralized place for sharing direct, clear, and reliable information about our projects.

Here, you may find:

- **Project Updates** — News about ongoing and upcoming projects.
- **Releases** — Announcements of new versions and features.
- **Maintenance Notices** — Information about maintenance and service interruptions.
- **Important Notices** — Changes, decisions, and other relevant updates.

## Guidelines

- This repository is intended exclusively for official announcements.
- Announcements should be clear, objective, and informative.
- Discussions, questions, and suggestions should be directed to the appropriate community channels.
- Only authorized maintainers should publish official announcements.
